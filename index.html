(About)

Architecture shaped by water and pine

Vesper occupies sixty-two acres of protected Jeffrey pine above a private stretch of shoreline. Every home faces the water, and none is visible from another.

We built fewer homes so each could be better: hand-oiled cedar, Sierra granite, glass that disappears in summer and walls that make winter feel like a choice. Concierge, ski valet and a lakeside spa are part of everyday life here, not an occasion.

(Our philosophy)

A life in tune with the lake

We believe a home should feel like the landscape it belongs to: calm, unhurried and built to last for generations.

  1. 01

    Stillness

    Rooms face the quietest views and are acoustically separated, so silence is part of the architecture.

  2. 02

    Privacy

    Gated grounds, a staffed entrance and homes screened by old-growth pine. Discretion is designed in, not added later.

  3. 03

    Craft

    Hand-oiled cedar, Sierra granite and bronze hardware, chosen to age gracefully rather than stay new.

  4. 04

    Community

    A small circle of owners, shared suppers at the boathouse and a concierge who knows your name.

  5. 05

    Stewardship

    Land held in conservation, solar-assisted energy and a shoreline restored with native planting.

Home is a reflection of what we value. At Vesper that means light over spectacle, nature over noise and time spent well.
